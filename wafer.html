Wafer inspection chamber

Wafer inspection is a core step in semiconductor manufacturing, and electron-beam review tools operate under vacuum. I’m designing a low-pressure chamber to prototype the idea at small scale with 3D-printed parts.

What I studied

KLA eDR7380 is an electron-beam system that images wafer defects at high resolution and sorts them with machine learning. Applied Materials HawkEye uses brightfield and darkfield optics with a deep-ultraviolet laser to find particles, pattern defects and scratches. I also compared how commercial machines enclose the wafer stage, load wafers and show results.
