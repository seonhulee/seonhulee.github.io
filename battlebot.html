Battlebot

We started with an off-the-shelf 4WD RC car under 1 kg and had to add 3D-printed parts that could beat cars built from the same base, inside SPARC safety rules. Our car placed 3rd of 25 teams, and no part broke during the competition.

My part

I modeled the chassis, the front and rear motor covers, the wheel geometry, the battery holder and a new base in SolidWorks. When parts didn’t fit, I re-measured the car and revised the dimensions.

How the design changed

Drag the handle to compare our first CAD with the car we ran on fight day. Every change came from test driving or FEA.

First designFight day
  • Ramp moved to the rearAt the front its weight made the car drag. At the rear, steering stays free.
  • Larger wheel cutoutsSide pieces removed and 70 × 70 mm cutouts added at the front wheels, which were dragging.
  • Glued tire protectorsCut from four to two and glued instead of bolted, after FEA showed stress at the bolt holes.
  • Blades at the frontMoved from the sides to the front and set vertically, where they cushion hits and catch opponents.

Parts I modeled

Each part was modeled in SolidWorks and dimensioned to fit the stock car.
